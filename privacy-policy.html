Evolv Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Evolv is a visual health and weight-journey app. This policy explains what the app handles, what normally stays on your Apple devices, what leaves your device when you choose connected features, and the controls available to you.

The short version

Evolv is local-first. Your journal, progress photos, detailed Health data, medication and peptide records, drink history, check-ins, and saved insights are stored on your device. They are not automatically uploaded to an Evolv account.

Data leaves your device only for features you choose, such as Sign in with Apple, AI Insights, Apple Weather, App Attest, or sharing an export. AI requests use a minimized summary and never include progress photos, freeform notes, custom drink names, raw HealthKit samples, or Medical Export files.

Information stored on your device

Depending on the features you use, Evolv may store:

  • progress photos, thumbnails, weights, goals, journey entries, comparisons, recaps, and achievements;
  • check-ins such as hunger, food noise, energy, protein, fiber, calories, activity, and selected side effects;
  • medication or peptide names, dose amounts, routes, schedules, reminders, and dose logs;
  • drink names, types, serving amounts, hydration goals, and caffeine information;
  • Health data you authorize Evolv to read, including supported weight, nutrition, activity, sleep, heart-rate, workout, and blood-pressure information;
  • saved AI Insights, app preferences, notification settings, and app-lock settings.

Evolv does not use CloudKit to sync this journal. Apple controls device backups and Apple Health storage. Protect your device and backups using Apple’s security settings.

Apple Health

If you grant permission, Evolv reads only the Apple Health categories you approve. The app uses this information locally to build daily summaries, show progress, match measurements to journey entries, populate Insights and exports, and prepare minimized AI context when you request an AI Insight.

If you separately enable “Save Drinks to Apple Health,” Evolv writes dietary water and, when present, caffeine for drinks logged through Evolv. Evolv uses sync identifiers to avoid duplicates and to remove the matching Health samples when you delete a synced drink.

Evolv does not use Health data for advertising, data brokerage, or cross-app tracking. You can review or revoke Health permissions in Apple Health or iPhone Settings at any time.

Progress photos and media

Progress photos and derived thumbnails remain on your device and are not sent to Evolv’s backend or the AI provider. If you save media to Photos or share a comparison, timelapse, recap, achievement, or export, the selected content leaves Evolv only through the destination you choose.

Location and Apple Weather

With optional When In Use location permission, Evolv requests a coarse current location (approximately three-kilometer accuracy) to obtain local weather from Apple Weather and adjust hydration guidance. Evolv caches the resulting temperature, humidity, forecast context, and Apple Weather attribution locally for up to three hours. Evolv does not send your location to the Evolv backend or include it in AI requests.

Apple Watch, widgets, and notifications

If you use the Apple Watch app or widgets, Evolv shares the minimum local snapshot needed with its companion app extensions. Watch entries synchronize with the paired iPhone using Apple’s device services. Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device.

Optional Evolv account and Sign in with Apple

You may create an Evolv account using Sign in with Apple to access connected features such as AI Insights. Evolv’s backend receives Apple’s sign-in credential and may store an Apple-linked account identifier, your email address and display name if Apple provides them, account creation information, session records, plan and entitlement status, and feature-usage limits. The app stores its session token in the iOS Keychain.

Creating an account does not upload your local journal, photos, raw Health data, medication history, drink history, or exports. Signing out ends the local session but does not erase your on-device journal.

AI Insights

AI Insights are optional and run only when you request one. Evolv prepares a compact structured snapshot that may contain dates, weight and trend summaries, structured check-in values, summarized Health-derived values, and structured medication or dose context you chose to record. The snapshot is sent to Evolv’s backend, which sends it to OpenAI’s API to generate the response.

The AI snapshot does not include:

  • photos, images, or media files;
  • your email address or Sign in with Apple identity;
  • freeform notes, detailed symptom notes, or custom drink names;
  • raw Apple Health samples or full lifetime data arrays;
  • Medical Export files, biometric authentication data, or security keys.

Evolv does not intentionally retain AI request snapshots or generated responses on its backend after processing. A response you save is stored locally on your device. OpenAI processes API data under its applicable service terms and privacy commitments. AI output can be incomplete or incorrect and is not medical advice.

Medical Export

Medical Export is assembled on your device from information you select. Evolv does not automatically upload the export. It leaves your device only when you copy, save, print, or share it using an Apple share destination you choose.

Security and service providers

Evolv uses Apple App Attest to help verify legitimate app requests. The private attestation key remains protected by Apple on your device. Evolv’s backend may store the associated public security record and operational request logs needed to protect the service, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, and enforce usage limits.

Connected features may use:

  • Apple for Sign in with Apple, HealthKit, WeatherKit, App Attest, Watch connectivity, notifications, and system sharing;
  • OpenAI to process minimized AI Insight requests;
  • Render to host Evolv’s account and AI-request backend.

No advertising or tracking

Evolv does not sell your personal information, show third-party advertising, use advertising identifiers, or track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Retention and deletion

Your local journal remains until you delete entries, delete the app, or erase the device data. Apple Health records remain under Apple Health’s controls.

Backend account, entitlement, security, session, and usage records are retained while needed to provide and secure connected services and meet legal obligations. You can permanently delete your Evolv account from the account settings in the app. Account deletion removes the Evolv account, server sessions, AI access record, and backend usage history. It does not delete your local journal or Apple Health data; remove those separately on your device.

Operational logs may persist for a limited period in service-provider systems for security and reliability. Data may be retained longer when required by law, to resolve disputes, or to prevent fraud and abuse.

Your choices

  • Choose which Apple Health permissions to grant or revoke.
  • Turn Apple Health drink writing, weather access, reminders, widgets, and app lock on or off.
  • Use Evolv’s local features without generating AI Insights.
  • Sign out of or permanently delete your optional Evolv account in the app.
  • Delete local entries or uninstall Evolv to remove its local app data.

Children

Evolv is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through a connected feature, contact us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Evolv’s features or service providers change. The “Last updated” date shows when the policy was revised.
