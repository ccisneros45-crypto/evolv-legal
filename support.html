Help with your visual progress journey, Apple Health, iCloud recovery, and Evolv Plus.

Need help? Email c.cisneros45@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Please do not email progress photos, medical records, passwords, or Apple Account credentials.

Apple Health

You control Health permissions in the Health app or iPhone Settings. Evolv works without Apple Health, but connected data can add weight, sleep, activity, nutrition, heart-rate, and blood-pressure context where available.

iCloud recovery

Evolv uses your private iCloud account for recovery when available and falls back to local storage if iCloud is temporarily unavailable. Check that you are signed into iCloud and that iCloud Drive is enabled for Evolv before switching devices.

Evolv Plus

Manage or cancel your subscription in Apple Account settings. To recover an eligible purchase, open Evolv, go to Profile → Evolv Plus, and choose Restore Purchases.

Account and privacy

Core tracking works without an Evolv account. Sign in with Apple is used for connected services such as AI Coach. You can sign out or delete the optional backend account from Evolv’s account settings without deleting the local Journey.

Read the Evolv Privacy Policy for details.
